    How the picker chooses
    • Each job keeps its own count. The fewer times you have cleaned the bathroom, the bigger your chance of getting the bathroom. The same goes for the room.
    • For every time you are ahead of whoever has done a job least, your chance of that job drops to less than half.
    • If you did a job last time, you are much less likely to get it again. Anyone who cleaned last time also gets a smaller break on the other job.
    • It is still a random draw. The bars under each name show their real chances for the next pick.
    • New housemates, and anyone who moves back in, start at the house average, so they don't get every job for weeks.
    • Editing or deleting a log entry updates everyone's chances straight away.
